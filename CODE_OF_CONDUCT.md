# Code of Conduct

Aura welcomes technical disagreement and rigorous review. Compiler and formal-methods work benefits from criticism that is specific, reproducible and directed at ideas or code rather than people.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss people respectfully,
- critique claims with evidence,
- avoid harassment, threats and discriminatory conduct,
- keep security reports private until coordinated disclosure,
- avoid flooding issues or reviews with repetitive/off-topic content,
- respect maintainers' decisions about scope and release readiness.

Technical claims may be challenged aggressively **on the evidence**. Personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

For private conduct concerns, contact the repository owner through an appropriate private GitHub channel rather than publishing sensitive personal information.
